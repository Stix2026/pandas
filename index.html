Areas of focus

Three areas we know deeply

Philosophy

How we practice

PANDAS and PANS can be confusing and, at times, contested territory — even among specialists. Our job is to give you a clear, honest account of the evidence, and a plan that fits your child, not a headline.

  • Evidence

    Grounded in current research

    Diagnosis and treatment follow current clinical criteria, updated as the research on PANDAS/PANS evolves.

  • Honest

    No overpromising

    We're clear about what treatment can realistically do, and we don't promise a guaranteed cure or timeline.

  • Whole child

    Built around your kid, not a label

    Care plans respect your child's whole neurodivergent profile, not just the symptom that brought you in.

  • Collaborative

    Works with your care team

    We coordinate with neurologists, immunologists, therapists, and your child's school so care fits the bigger picture.

Getting started

What working together looks like

Comprehensive evaluation

A detailed history, symptom timeline, relevant labs, and review of prior infections or treatments already tried.

A clear, honest plan

A written plan laying out the recommended next steps, what each one is meant to do, and what we'll watch for.

Ongoing monitoring & adjustment

Regular check-ins to track flares, response to treatment, and adjust the plan as your child changes.
